Capstone projectIndividualApex Health Network

Closed-Loop Medication & Pharmacy Optimisation

Problem
Medication management at Apex Health Network relied on disconnected systems and manual processes. The case identified a high medication error rate, no real-time inventory visibility leading to critical stockouts, paper-based prescribing and controlled-drug tracking, expiry and waste, financial leakage, and unreliable analytics.
My contribution
Project initiation and problem analysis, including root cause analysis. Stakeholder analysis (power/interest matrix and engagement strategy), RACI and communication matrices. The business case: options appraisal, expected benefits, cost estimate and risk register, plus a recommended SDLC approach.
Approach
Options appraisal, cost-benefit and risk assessment with mitigations, and a hybrid Waterfall and Agile SDLC. Proposed solution: barcode medication administration with eMAR, real-time inventory, expiry tracking, a digital audit trail, billing integration and a management dashboard.
Outcome
A business case presentation comparing three options and recommending an integrated closed-loop medication platform over doing nothing or upgrading existing systems. It sets out a planning-assumption investment of £7.75m over a 12-month implementation and a ten-item risk register (including staff resistance, integration failure and data privacy).

Volunteer projectPod team10alytics

Drug Delivery Process Improvement

Problem
Routine delivery takes up to 7 days against a 3-day target. Capture errors affect 12% of orders and staff can see the status of only 40%, creating follow-up work and uncertain delivery expectations. The aim was an integrated digital order management and tracking process.
My contribution
Team project with my pod members. Together we carried out gap analysis, fishbone root cause analysis, AS-IS and TO-BE process mapping, the problem statement, objectives and scope, MoSCoW prioritisation, stakeholder analysis, and functional and non-functional requirements.
Approach
Fishbone across six categories, AS-IS and TO-BE swim lane maps (Draw.io), MoSCoW prioritisation, stakeholder grid, RAG indicators, KPI dashboard design, and RACI and SOP planning.
Outcome
A Business Requirements Document (v0.1, 23 October 2026, issued for approval): 4 prioritised objectives, 9 business requirements, 8 stakeholder requirements, and 17 functional and 8 non-functional requirements. Proposed targets are delivery in 3 days or less by week 12, 100% order tracking and fewer errors (0% proposed, to be agreed with management).
